# Recognize ration variants and forage reviewed floor food

## Actionable facts

- A held, partly-eaten identified ration/fruit is still itself; resume eating it.
- Collect an identified reviewed comestible on a non-shop floor cell cheaply reachable while unburdened; otherwise eat it only at Hungry+, confirming the exact offer.
- Garlic nourishes normally, but an undead hero only vomits from one; skip it while undead. (Comestible rev 2026-05-23; Food ration rev 2026-08-28; Clove of garlic rev 2024-07-14)

## Non-goals

- No unreviewed comestible, no picking up while encumbered, no relaxed corpse rules.
